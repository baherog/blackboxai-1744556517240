Neon Dodger

Move left/right, dodge hazards, and chase a new high score.

How to play

You are the neon block near the bottom. Avoid the falling hazards for as long as possible.

  • ←/→ or A/D: move
  • P: pause / resume
  • Space: start (or restart after game over)
  • R: restart

Tip: The game speeds up over time. Stay calm and make small movements.

Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS. Open index.html to play.
